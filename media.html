What newsrooms are dealing with

Readers never see the homepage

They arrive from a phone, through a feed or a search result, straight onto one article. The front page you spend the most time on is the page fewest people load.

Subscription revenue has to be built

It does not arrive because the journalism is good. It needs a paywall that works, a reason to register, and something worth paying for on the other side.

Small teams, more formats than ever

The same newsroom is now expected to produce text, audio, video, vertical clips, newsletters and social - without growing.

Decades of material nobody can search

The archive holds the answer to half the questions an editor asks, and no practical way to get at it.

Reach readers on mobile

Native iOS and Android apps with paywalls, e-paper, personalisation and push — published under your own brand.

AI in the editorial workflow

SEO suggestions and live trend checks while an article is being written, auto-tagging from your own taxonomy, comment moderation, and social posts and newsletters generated from published articles. These sit inside mPanel CMS and Fronisa — there is nothing extra to buy or bolt on.
