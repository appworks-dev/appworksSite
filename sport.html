What sports organisations are dealing with

Fans follow you somewhere else

Most of the relationship now happens on a phone, through feeds you do not own and cannot measure. Your own channels see a fraction of it.

Commercial needs something it can sell

Sponsors ask for reach and proof. Without measurable inventory of your own, the commercial department is negotiating on goodwill.

The archive is effectively closed

Decades of photo and video, and no way to find the one clip you need without someone scrolling through folders.

Nobody has the full picture

Press, online, broadcast and the fan forums all say things about the club, the squad and the coach. Almost none of it reaches you in one place.

What it looks like in practice

How our partners work

FK Partizan

FK Partizan runs its website, CMS, mobile app, transcription and media archive through one platform. A photographer uploads from the pitch and the image is ready to publish and share with media within minutes; a press conference becomes an article and a set of subtitled clips the same afternoon. One partner across the whole digital operation, rather than five vendors and the integration work between them.

Greek Basketball League

Leagues face the club problem multiplied: fixtures, results and standings across dozens of teams, and fans who want all of it live. The Greek Basket League app runs largely on autopilot: articles come from the league’s own website, live scores and statistics sync straight from the data provider, and gamification keeps fans engaged between matchdays. Nobody on staff updates anything by hand.

Read the story

Croatian Basketball Federation

The Croatian Basketball Association covers men’s and women’s leagues, junior competitions and the national teams, and each fan follows only part of that. Games, standings, box scores and play-by-play arrive from the data provider, news and video from the federation’s own channels, and notifications are switchable per competition, so following one league doesn’t mean hearing about all of them. Live match alerts update in place instead of firing again for every basket.

Read the story

Serbian Football League

Fans of Serbia’s top football division were assembling a matchday out of club sites and social media. The official league app puts fixtures, live results, the table and club stats in one place, with a news feed from every club in the league and Fantasy integrated from day one, after three years without it in Serbia. Notifications follow the clubs a fan actually picks, so there is a reason to open the app between matchdays too.
