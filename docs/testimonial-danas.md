# Danas testimonial — original and translation

Supplied by the client on 2026-08-07 for the homepage (replacing Politika's) and
for the full list on `partners.html`. Every other testimonial on the site is in the
partner's own words in English; this one is a translation, so the original is kept
here to check it against.

## Original (Serbian)

> "Saradnja sa App Works-om za nas je izuzetno korisna i efikasna. Program Littera
> našim novinarima ne samo da štedi vreme, omogućavajući im da se temeljnije posvete
> radu na tekstovima, već im pruža i sigurnost da je transkript precizan i pouzdan.
> To je posebno važno danas, kada je brzina objavljivanja informacija jedan od
> ključnih faktora za vidljivost medija."

## As published (English)

> "Working with App Works has been extremely useful and efficient for us. Littera
> not only saves our journalists time, letting them give the writing itself more
> attention, but also gives them the confidence that the transcript is precise and
> reliable. That matters especially today, when the speed at which information is
> published is one of the key factors in a media outlet's visibility."

## Translation choices worth confirming

- **"Littera"** is kept as written. The original says *Program Littera*; the
  product's public name on this site is **Litteraworks**, and every other
  testimonial that mentions it uses that name. Changing it would read more
  consistently but would also be putting a word in the partner's mouth, so it is
  left alone pending a decision.
- **"App Works"** is kept as two words, as in the original. The site uses both
  *Appworks* and *App Works* across existing quotes, so this is consistent with what
  is already live.
- **"vidljivost medija"** is rendered "a media outlet's visibility" rather than
  "media visibility", which in English reads as press coverage of someone else
  rather than the outlet's own reach.
- The wordplay in the original is not reproducible: *danas* means "today", so
  "posebno važno danas" contains the paper's own name. In English it is just "today".

## Attribution

No individual name or role was supplied, so both cards are attributed to **Danas /
