# SEO audit: app-works.app

**Date:** 2026-10-30. **State audited:** `main` at `929b554`. The checked pages on WEB2 and
WEB4 match each other and the repo byte for byte.

**Method:** crawled all 75 sitemap URLs plus `/fronisa.html` and `/404.html` as Googlebot,
rendered 5 pages in headless Chromium, ran Lighthouse locally on 4 pages, and tested links
and legacy URLs. Search data is from April 2026 (see "What this audit could not see").

## Verdict

The technical foundation is sound. The weak areas are how articles link to each other,
article metadata, page speed, and content aimed at non-brand searches. In the January to
April data, every click on a listed query was a brand search.

## Fix first

### 1. litteraworks.com is down (outage, not SEO)
It returns 503 from DEV, directly from WEB2, and from an outside fetch. `/` and
`/about.html` link to it, and articles in the `litteraworks-com` category return 410 here
because they are meant to live there. Those articles are currently unavailable on both sites.

**Cause:** its vhost is the only one of 179 PHP handlers on WEB2 using the shared
`/run/php/php-fpm.sock`. That symlink resolves to `/run/php/php7.4-fpm.sock`, and `php7.4-fpm`
is inactive and disabled, so every PHP page fails while static files still load. The first
error is 22 October 2026 at 09:45 CET. The site has no PHP 8.2 pool of its own.

**Fixed 30 October:** the site now has its own PHP 8.2.35 pool, as user `litteraw`. `MultiViews` was
also removed from the vhost, because it made every `/article/N` URL return 404, including before the
outage. All 22 sitemap URLs return 200. Vhost backup: `/root/backups/litteraworks-php82-*` on WEB2.

### 2. Article metadata: 40 of 62 descriptions can be fixed in one template change
- `article.php:217` prints the CMS `intro` untruncated, or the title if there is no intro.
- **29 articles** have a description over 160 characters (the longest is 700).
- **11 articles** have a description that just repeats the title.
- **53 of 62** titles exceed 60 characters, 31 exceed 70, and 12 are 90 or more.
- **`article.php` never reads `seo_title` or `seo_description`.** Overrides come only from
  the hardcoded `$seoOverrides` array (`article.php:18`), so filling those CMS fields, as
  `RECOMMENDATIONS-CONTENT.md` §6 advises, currently does nothing.
- Only one article has `seo_title` set in the 50 returned by the listing, and it is cut off
  ("How AI Processes Unstructured Evidence for Civic."). Fix it before the template reads
  that field. `seo_description` is empty on all 50. The other 15 of the 65 total were not sampled.

**Fix:** read the CMS SEO fields first, then the override array, then a description cut at a
word boundary near 155 characters, using the article's first paragraph when the intro is
empty or repeats the title.

### 3. Internal links leave older articles isolated
- `/insights` shows **11 of 62** article links after rendering. The rest appear only in a
  `<noscript>` list (`articles.php:939`), and "Load More" is a `<button>` with no link
  (`articles.php:610`).
- **25 articles** have that noscript list as their only inbound link in the raw HTML of the
  crawled pages. **32** have no link from any other article.
- The related-articles module draws from the newest 8 in the category, then the newest 12
  overall (`article.php:343-346`), so older articles rarely get picked.
- **48 of 62** article bodies contain no internal link, and **none** links to a product page
  from the body. The template CTA below the article is the only such link.

**Severity check:** this is a ranking-strength problem, not an indexing failure. All 18 of the
noscript-only articles that were inspected in April were indexed. Whether Google follows the
noscript links is unverified, so don't rely on them.

**Fix:** server-render the first page of `/insights`, add real paginated links, widen the
related-articles pool beyond the newest articles, and add body links from articles to the
matching product pages.

### 4. Page speed misses Google's LCP target on every page tested
Lighthouse, simulated mobile, single run from DEV. These are lab figures, not real-user data.
The LCP target is 2.5 s and the CLS target is 0.1.

| Page | Perf | LCP | CLS | TBT | Weight |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| `/` | 61 | 6.9 s | 0.095 | 250 ms | 3,396 KiB |
| `/fanfuel.html` | 63 | 7.2 s | 0.122 | 220 ms | 2,724 KiB |
| `/insights` | 65 | 10.2 s | 0.089 | 190 ms | 2,455 KiB |
| Flagship AI newsroom article | 71 | 4.9 s | 0.043 | 240 ms | 1,867 KiB |

Main causes, largest first:
- **Images:** Lighthouse estimates 1.3 to 2.3 MB of savings per page. The flagship article's
  image is a 1.37 MB PNG, and the home and FanFuel pages load PNGs of 430 to 610 KB from mPanel.
- **Render-blocking CSS:** Google Fonts and bootstrap-icons from jsDelivr, estimated at about
  0.8 to 0.95 s each.
- **`/api/articles.php`:** 194 KB of uncompressed JSON for 12 cards on `/insights`, and it is
  also uncompressed on the homepage.

### 5. Sitemap `lastmod` reports the publish date on all 62 articles
`sitemap.php:69` reads `updated_at`, which the listing endpoint does not return, so it falls
back to `created_at`. **48 articles** have a `lastmod` older than the `dateModified` in their own
schema, in some cases by more than three years. So edits such as the 6 August 2026 batch are
not signalled to Google.

Switching to `update_date` will not fix it: that field matched the creation day on all 50
articles sampled. Either the CMS must expose the real value in the listing, or the sitemap must
fetch each article and cache the result.

### 6. Commercial pages name the product more than the category
- `/fanfuel.html` has the H1 "FanFuel". "Fan engagement" appears 3 times and in no H2.
- `/verbavista.html` never uses the phrase "news app".
- `/cms.html` has the H1 "mPanel CMS", 507 words, and no links to case-study articles.
- None of the 62 articles is a buyer's guide for "fan engagement app", the only real non-brand
  cluster in the data: 184 impressions at position 13.8 for the exact query from January to April.

## Low priority

- **Schema:** `contact.html` uses `LocalBusiness` under the same `@id` as the homepage's
  `Organization`, and spells the street differently ("Vlajkovićeva" and "Vlajkoviceva").
  `applicationCategory: "MobileApplication"` is not a useful category. All 62 articles credit
  the organisation rather than a named author, which is a missed credibility signal for B2B.
- **Social sharing:** the 14 static pages (including `/fronisa.html`) share the logo as `og:image`.
- **Stray public files:** `/server-root-index.html` redirects to the dead `/appworksSite/home.html`.
  `/package.json`, `/tailwind.config.js` and `/api/articles.php` are publicly fetchable.
- **Missing fallback image:** `/images/placeholder.jpg`, used by `onerror` on `/insights`, returns 404.
- **Stray request:** article pages make an image-type request to `/`, downloading about 126 KB of
  HTML. The source was not found in the DOM or CSS checks.

## What is working

- All 75 sitemap URLs return 200 with a self-referencing canonical and no `noindex`.
- No duplicate titles, descriptions or H1s. One H1 per page except one article with three.
- All JSON-LD parses; Article and BreadcrumbList appear on every article.
- HTTP to HTTPS and `www` to non-`www` redirects work. Unknown URLs return 404, and hidden articles return 410.
- Googlebot, Bingbot, GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot user agents all get 200. This tests the
  user-agent string only, not real crawler IP addresses.
- Lighthouse SEO score is 100 on all 4 pages tested. That confirms the basics only.
- Done since the August audit: product pages indexable, real 404s, ISO dates in schema, compiled Tailwind, asset caching.

## Stale documents

- `TASKS-TECH.md` T2 says to remove the product pages from the sitemap. They have since had
  `noindex` lifted and belong there, so don't follow T2.
- `RECOMMENDATIONS-CONTENT.md` §6: the `seo_title` backfill has no effect until item 2 above ships.

## What this audit could not see

- **Current rankings, queries and index status.** The newest data here is the 22 April export and
  a 10 April Tarkin snapshot on DEV. Those snapshots predate the product pages being indexed.
- **Tarkin's daily cache on QIRA.** `sc-domain:app-works.app` is configured in Tarkin's
  `gsc_instances.json`, so `/home/tarkin/gsc_data/` on QIRA may hold current data. Reading it over
  SSH was blocked by the session's production-read permission rule, so it was not checked.
- **Real-user Core Web Vitals.** The PageSpeed API quota was exhausted, so there is no field data.
- **Backlinks.** No backlink data source was available.
- **Google's own view of the pages.** Rendering used headless Chromium with a Googlebot user agent,
  which approximates Google's renderer. A web search for the site returned nothing from the domain,
  but that tool is not Google, so it says nothing about indexing.
